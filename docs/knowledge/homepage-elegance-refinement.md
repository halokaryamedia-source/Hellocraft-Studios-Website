## Finding

The controlled rebuild removed the strongest generative-design tells, but the result remained too neutral and visually flat. The next correction therefore improves proportion, tonal depth, media presentation, and hierarchy without restoring decorative gimmicks.

## Adopted corrections

- keep one dominant Hero statement, but give supporting copy a deliberate side-column relationship;
- use a quieter neutral palette with one cyan accent;
- make placeholder media visually composed through simple brand-colored planes rather than empty black/gray boxes;
- keep Featured Work clearly dominant and make the second study materially smaller;
- use a softly differentiated Studio surface rather than another full dark chapter;
- make the closing Contact surface a contained dark panel instead of another page-sized section;
- use sentence-case UI language and restrained link treatment;
- preserve the existing content owners and route architecture.

## Guardrails

Do not reintroduce editorial rails, cut corners, HUD/coordinate labels, fake metrics, decorative stars, bento grids, gradient glow, or multiple competing display moments.

The target is not novelty. The target is a page that feels art-directed because its proportions, typography, spacing, and media hierarchy are deliberate.
